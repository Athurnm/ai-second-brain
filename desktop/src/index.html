                What is a terminal?
                

                  A terminal is a text-based window for typing commands directly to your computer.
                  On Windows, search for "Terminal" or "PowerShell" in the Start menu. On a Mac,
                  search for "Terminal" with Spotlight (Cmd+Space). On Linux, look for "Terminal" in
                  your applications menu. Paste the command above into it and press Enter.
